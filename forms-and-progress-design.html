Design notes

  • One engine, two kinds. The builder's segmented control flips a FormTemplate.kind between interview and evaluation — identical card model, so the team learns it once.
  • Every question = score + comment. A 1–5 Likert (blue active pill) plus a per-question comment field, surfaced on the card and snapshotted into each response so editing the template never rewrites a student's history.
  • Read vs write, made obvious. The admin builder uses magenta Save/Add and editable cards; the student profile is calm and read-only (lock/calendar meta-bands, locked score pips) — same tokens, different posture.
  • Numbers always mono. Scores, averages, percentages, deltas and chart axes use JetBrains Mono; trend deltas read green when up — consistent with the attendance mockup.
  • Progress at a glance. A blue score line over the 4 cycles (3.2 → 4.4) sits above an attendance bar trend on the same period, then a tappable per-cycle list drilling into question-level scores and comments.
