Designed for the field
  • Mobile-first. The phone layout is the primary one — master/detail collapses to a drill-down stack, so deep trees never cramp.
  • Sunlight-legible. High-contrast text, larger type, solid fills over subtle grays — readable in bright outdoor light.
  • Thumb-first. Primary actions (Add, Assign, Save) pinned to the bottom; tap targets ≥ 44px for use while standing or moving.
  • Works offline. Spotty signal outdoors → read from cache, writes queue locally and sync when back online. Most critical for attendance marking, the real on-the-go task.
  • Same data & permissions — only the layout reflows.
