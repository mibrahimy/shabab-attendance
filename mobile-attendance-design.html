What changed in the polish pass

  • Real icon set. Inline stroke icons (back, search, chevrons, tab bar, check, alert) replace the emoji glyphs — the biggest "templated" tell, gone.
  • Refined depth & rhythm. Layered soft shadows, hairline borders, an 8px spacing system, consistent radii — instead of flat default cards.
  • Cleaner marking control. Calmer segmented pills with status-coloured fills + soft glow, no gimmicky scale.
  • Logo-driven brand. Headers carry the logo's own blue→magenta gradient (so they match the mark); the UI — buttons, links, selection — stays royal blue. Status is unambiguous: present green, late amber, absent red, excused slate.
  • Soft surface for sun. Broad areas use a soft off-white (not pure white) to cut glare; pure white is reserved for elevated cards so they still pop. Light scheme, kept high-contrast — better outdoors than dark.
